## Topic outline (politician checklist, adapted)

1. Personal background — birth (21 May 1944, Ballina, Co. Mayo), family (Bourke; both parents medical doctors), education in Dublin and at Harvard.
2. Education — Mount Anville; Trinity College Dublin (LLB 1967); King's Inns; Harvard LLM 1968.
3. Pre-political career — barrister 1967; Reid Professor of Constitutional Law TCD from 1969.
4. Entry into politics — appointed (TCD Senator) to Seanad Éireann 1969 at age 25.
5. Constituencies / elected positions — Seanad 1969–1989 (TCD seat); Dublin Corporation councillor 1979–1983; President of Ireland 1990–1997.
6. Roles held — President of Ireland; UN High Commissioner for Human Rights 1997–2002; UN Special Envoy roles 2013–2016; Chair of The Elders 2018–2024.
7. Major legislative positions — contraception bills (1971 onwards), divorce, decriminalisation of homosexuality, women's rights.
8. Defections / party switches — Robinson resigned from the Labour Party in 1985 over the Anglo-Irish Agreement (note: confirm date; she was a Labour senator and resigned over Anglo-Irish Agreement).
9. Electoral history — 1990 presidential election won on second count with ~52% after transfers; first count 38.9%.
10. Controversies and legal matters — Durban 2001 controversy; Princess Latifa / Sheikha Latifa Al Maktoum 2018 visit and 2021 acknowledgement of error.
11. Stated ideology and policy positions — human rights, women's rights, climate justice, anti-poverty.
12. Family in politics — none directly.
13. Recent activity (last 24 months) — COP28/COP29 commentary; Elders chair until October 2024; criticism of Trump energy policy January 2025.
14. Public perception — credited with modernising the Irish presidency; criticised over Durban and Latifa episodes.

## Contradictions found

- Wikipedia and OHCHR both say her tenure as UN HCHR ran 1997–2002. Some secondary sources phrase it as "from 1997 to 2002" without the day-precise dates (took office 12 September 1997, resigned post effective 11 September 2002).
- Date of climate envoy role: variously described as 2013–2016 vs. 2014–2015. The Elders profile says she was Special Envoy for the Great Lakes (2013), then on Climate Change, then on El Niño and Climate. Reuters / UN profile pages dated her climate envoy role as starting 2014. Treat as a sequence of envoy roles 2013–2016, with the climate envoy designation specifically c. 2014–2016.
