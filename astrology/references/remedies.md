# Remedies — distilled from Textbook Chapter 34

Textbook Chapter 34, "Remedial Measures," pp.450–459. This file is a structured index; always open the source pages to confirm wording before citing.

## Rao's framing (Textbook §34.1, p.450)

> "The pattern to be followed by one's life is decided at the time of one's birth … However, it only decides the basic 'pattern' and not all the minute details … Actions taken by one's free will have a role, along with accumulated karma of past lives, in deciding the exact details. Remedial measures are the measures we take in order to manipulate the exact details to be relatively favorable to us."

Tone cue: remedies are *modulators of detail*, not magic erasers of karma. The reading's remedies section should reflect this.

**Rao also writes**: "Those who have *no* belief in Vedic remedies may skip this chapter" (p.450). If the user signals disbelief, offer remedies in an "if this tradition speaks to you" framing — don't push.

## The four methods of planetary pacification (§34.1.2, p.451)

1. **Wearing a gemstone** — works only for *favorable* planets in the chart. Wearing a gemstone of a functional malefic is harmful.
2. **Good deeds** — committing acts in the domain the planet rules.
3. **Planetary propitiation** — mantras, stotras.
4. **Propitiation of deities** — worshiping the ruling deity of the planet.

Always frame the remedies you prescribe within this fourfold structure. Cite as `[Textbook §34.1.2, p.451]`.

| Ketu | Cat's eye | Silver |

Cite `[Textbook §34.2 Table 77, p.452]`.

**Selection rules**:
- Prescribe a gemstone only for a *functional benefic* in the chart, or a yogakaraka, or a planet whose dasha is running (Textbook §34.2, p.451).
- **Avoid** gemstones of functional malefics. Avoid maraka gemstones during maraka dashas.
- If one wants improvement in a specific life area, pick the yogakaraka of the relevant divisional chart (e.g., D-10 yogakaraka for career).
- Example from the book: if lagna is Capricorn and Venus (yogakaraka) is in 8th (Leo), a diamond strengthens Venus despite the awkward placement (p.451).

**Wearing instructions**:
- First worn on the weekday ruled by the planet. Planetary strength should be good at that time.
- Traditional finger assignments: Jupiter→index, Saturn→middle, Sun→ring, Mercury→little. Some assign fingers to the four purusharthas: thumb=dharma, ring=artha, little=kama, index=moksha — wear the ring on the finger representing the purushartha one wants to cultivate (p.453).
- Two gemstones can be combined when the corresponding planets are friends and both functional benefics forming a yoga. Examples from book (p.452): emerald + diamond for Virgo lagna (Mercury + Venus = dharma-karmadhipati yoga); ruby + pearl for Aries lagna (Sun + Moon).

Rao's framing on mantras (p.454): "A mantra should be recited with accurate pronunciation and intonation … Some mantras can produce negative effects with improper recitation. So one should be careful." Echo this in the reading.

**Weekday of recitation**: the weekday ruled by the planet is ideal — Sunday (Sun), Monday (Moon), Tuesday (Mars), Wednesday (Mercury), Thursday (Jupiter), Friday (Venus), Saturday (Saturn). Rahu and Ketu have no weekday ruler; Rao does not specify in this section.

Cite `[Textbook §34.5 Table 78, p.458]`.

**Choosing the specific form of the deity depends on chart context** (Rao, p.458). E.g., Rahu in Cancer might call for Durga; Rahu with Mars in Gemini and Saturn/Mercury aspects might suggest Ugra Narasimha (angry form of Vishnu). When recommending, pick the form that matches the planetary tenor in the native's chart, and cite Rao's explanation. Don't over-specify if the chart doesn't clearly support a particular form — name the primary deity and note "the particular form is best chosen in consultation with a priest."

**Divisional-chart-targeted deity worship** (p.457): "if one wants spiritual progress, lagna lord in D-20 should be worshipped. If one wants pleasures and comforts, lagna lord in D-16 should be worshipped."

**For liberation** (p.457): "Propitiation of the deity corresponding to the strongest planet occupying or owning or aspecting the 12th house from AK in D-9 takes one's soul closer to liberation."

## Common-sense remedies (§34.1.1, pp.450–451)

Rao lists these *first*, before the esoteric remedies. They matter:

- When a difficult period is anticipated, take precautions — drive carefully, avoid travel during accident-prone phases, restrain oneself during periods that indicate quarrels with authorities (p.450).
- Don't neglect medical care — astrology indicates tendencies; doctors treat.

Include at least one common-sense remedy in the reading when the chart or current dasha warrants caution. Cite `[Textbook §34.1.1, p.450]`.

## Building the remedies section of the reading

**Structure**:

1. **Opening framing** — 2-3 sentences drawing from §34.1 (p.450) about remedies modulating detail but not erasing karma. Note the "those who don't believe in this tradition may skip" framing (lightly, not off-puttingly).
2. **Gemstones** (if appropriate) — identify the chart's yogakaraka or strongest functional benefic. Prescribe that gemstone with metal and wearing instructions. Cite Table 77.
3. **Mantras** — 2-3 targeted mantras from §34.4, chosen for: (a) the lagna lord, (b) a weak/afflicted functional benefic that the chart needs, (c) the current dasha lord if afflicted. Include weekday and rough repetition count. Cite §34.4.X and the page.
4. **Deity worship** — 1-2 primary deities from Table 78 matched to the same planets as the mantras. Include the divisional-chart-targeted guidance if the native has expressed interest in a specific area (career → D-10 lagna lord's deity; spirituality → D-20 lagna lord's deity).
5. **Good deeds & charity** — 3-4 items from §34.3, tied to the planets the reading has emphasized.
6. **Common-sense precautions** — from §34.1.1 — targeted to periods flagged as difficult in the dasha forecast.
7. **Closing note** — "the exact application … depends on an astrologer's knowledge of mythology and classics and familiarity with the natures of various deities" (Textbook §34.5, p.458). Encourage the native to consult a priest or experienced jyotishi for final selection and installation (especially of a gemstone).

**Don't prescribe everything for everyone.** A targeted remedy set of 6-10 items tailored to *this* chart is more useful than a 50-item generic list. Show why each item was chosen.

**Rao closes Chapter 34 (§34.6, p.459)** pointing readers to Pt. Sanjay Rath's *Vedic Remedies in Astrology* for deeper study. If the user wants more, mention this.
