# Life-area synthesis framework

The Textbook teaches technique (houses, yogas, dashas) but does not itself synthesize a reading organized around "career", "marriage", "health" etc. The life-area section of the reading is *derived*: for each area, you combine a well-defined set of technical indicators from the source material.

**Every claim inside a life-area section must still cite the underlying technical source** — the synthesis itself is not a citeable fact, but each component claim (what the 10th house means, what Venus signifies, what a certain yoga does) is.

Below: for each life area, the indicators to combine, and where in the Textbook/Lessons each component is grounded.

---

## 1. Self & Personality

**Indicators to synthesize**
- 1st house (lagna) and its occupants
- Lagna lord — sign, house, dignity, aspects
- Moon's sign and house (the *manas* — mind and emotions)
- Sun's sign and house (the *atma* — soul and self-expression)
- Ascendant sign nature (movable/fixed/dual; fiery/earthy/airy/watery)

**Grounding**
- Houses/lord framework: `[Textbook Ch 7, p.67]`
- Moon as mind: `[Textbook Ch 3, p.28]` (planets chapter) and `[Textbook Ch 8, p.79]` (karakas)
- Sun as soul: same
- Sign natures: `[Textbook Ch 2, p.21]`

## Structuring the synthesis section in the PDF

Each life-area subsection in the final PDF should follow this shape:

1. **One-line verdict** (italic): "Your career trajectory is [strong/mixed/challenged/late-blooming]."
2. **2–4 paragraphs synthesizing the indicators**, with inline citations for each component claim.
3. **Current period outlook** (1 paragraph): how the active dasha/antardasha modulates this area's expression.
4. **Targeted remedies** (bulleted, 2–4 items): drawn from `remedies.md`; each cited to Textbook Ch 34.

The bulleted remedies block per area lets the user scan quickly, while the prose above grounds *why* those remedies are appropriate for this chart.
