# The twelve bhavas (houses)

Quick-reference for each house's karakas, primary significations, and where to look in the Textbook (Ch 7, pp.67–78, is the canonical source). Cite `[Textbook Ch 7, p.67]` for the chapter's general framing; cite finer pages when the Textbook gives specific effects.

For each house, consider (in order):

1. **Sign on the cusp** — colors the house's nature.
2. **House lord (bhava-esha)** — its sign, house placement, dignity, and aspects. A house's fortunes follow its lord. The lord in a kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) or trikona (1, 5, 9) is favorable; in dusthanas (6, 8, 12) usually undermines the house.
3. **Occupants** — planets tenanting the house. Benefics support; malefics afflict unless the house benefits from malefic energy (3rd, 6th, 10th, 11th).
4. **Aspects** — who aspects the house and its lord. Jupiter and yogakaraka aspects are strongly beneficial.
5. **Karaka** — the natural significator; a weak karaka undermines the house even if the house itself looks good.
6. **Divisional chart** — the relevant varga if available.

---

## 1st house — Lagna / Tanu Bhava
**Significations**: body, general health and vitality, personality, self, head, fame, disposition. The "house of the self."
**Karaka**: Sun (for soul), Ascendant itself.
**Key divisional chart**: Rasi is primary; D-1 is the lagna itself.
**Notes**: Lagna lord's strength is the single most important indicator of a life's overall trajectory.

## 2nd house — Dhana Bhava
**Significations**: accumulated wealth (as opposed to earned income — that's 11th), family of origin (kutumba), speech, face, eyes, food, values, eventual family lineage. Also a maraka (life-shortening) house.
**Karaka**: Jupiter (wealth); Mercury (speech).
**Key divisional chart**: None primary.
**Notes**: strong 2nd + strong 11th + strong Jupiter = wealth. 2nd lord placement tells the story of how wealth arrives.

## 3rd house — Sahaja Bhava
**Significations**: younger siblings, courage, initiative, short journeys, communications, skills of the hands, writing, media.
**Karaka**: Mars (courage), Mercury.
**Key divisional chart**: D-3 (Drekkana) for siblings.
**Notes**: an upachaya house — benefits from malefics. Malefics here often indicate courageous, self-made natives.

## 4th house — Sukha Bhava
**Significations**: mother, home, landed property, vehicles, education (general), emotional foundation, inner happiness, the heart.
**Karaka**: Moon (mother, emotions), Mercury (education).
**Key divisional chart**: D-4 (Chaturthamsa) for property; D-12 for parents.
**Notes**: the "core happiness" house — afflictions here undermine felt wellbeing even when external success looks fine.

## 5th house — Putra Bhava
**Significations**: children, intelligence, creativity, speculation and investments, purva-punya (accumulated merit from past lives), mantra-upasana (devotional practice), romance.
**Karaka**: Jupiter (children, wisdom), Mercury (intellect).
**Key divisional chart**: D-7 (Saptamsa) for children; D-20 for spiritual life.
**Notes**: a trikona (highly auspicious) house. Lord in a kendra → a prominent Raja Yoga (see Textbook Ch 11).

## 6th house — Ripu / Roga Bhava
**Significations**: enemies, disease, debt, litigation, obstacles overcome, daily work and service, pets.
**Karaka**: Mars (enemies), Saturn (disease, service).
**Notes**: an upachaya — benefits from malefics. Benefics here can weirdly empower enemies. A strong 6th lord in a good house → overcomes enemies, disease, debt. 6th lord in the 8th or 12th is classically called vipareeta raja yoga (cite Textbook Ch 11).

## 7th house — Kalatra Bhava
**Significations**: marriage partner, spouse, business partnerships, public self, foreign travel, open conflicts, sexual life. Also a maraka.
**Karaka**: Venus (for men — wife); Jupiter (for women — husband).
**Key divisional chart**: **D-9 (Navamsa) is essential here.** Rao emphasizes the 7th house analysis is incomplete without D-9 (Textbook Ch 6, pp.51–66).
**Notes**: also examine upapada lagna (UL, from Ch 9) for marriage nuance.

## 8th house — Ayur Bhava
**Significations**: longevity, chronic illness, transformations, inheritance, occult/hidden knowledge, research, death and rebirth, spouse's wealth (being 2nd from 7th).
**Karaka**: Saturn.
**Notes**: a dusthana. Afflictions here often show transformative but difficult episodes. Mars or Saturn here can indicate accidents/surgery. A strong 8th *lord in the 8th* (being in own house in a dusthana) is nuanced — see Textbook Ch 7. **Never predict death or specific health catastrophe** — keep hedged per the sensitive-topics guidance.

## 9th house — Bhagya Bhava
**Significations**: father, guru, religion, Dharma, fortune in general, long journeys, higher learning, philosophy, luck.
**Karaka**: Sun (father), Jupiter (guru, Dharma).
**Key divisional chart**: None primary; D-9 is named after it but relates to marriage + overall strength.
**Notes**: the second-most-important trikona after the 1st. Strong 9th = fortunate life, supportive father, righteous disposition.

## 10th house — Karma Bhava
**Significations**: career, profession, public image and reputation, authority, status, actions in the world. The most-visible house.
**Karaka**: Sun (authority), Mercury, Jupiter, and **Saturn** (Rao treats Saturn as karaka for *work* / labor in the 10th house sense).
**Key divisional chart**: **D-10 (Dasamsa) is essential.** Analysis of career without D-10 is preliminary only (Textbook Ch 6).
**Notes**: aspects from benefics on 10th house/lord → prestige. Yogakaraka placements involving the 10th → Raja Yoga.

## 11th house — Labha Bhava
**Significations**: gains (earned income, as opposed to accumulated wealth of 2nd), elder siblings, friends, social circles, aspirations fulfilled, desires met.
**Karaka**: Jupiter.
**Notes**: an upachaya. Strong 11th + strong 2nd → wealth that arrives *and* sticks. 11th lord placement shows how income flows.

## 12th house — Vyaya Bhava
**Significations**: losses and expenditures, foreign lands, spirituality and liberation (moksha), secluded places (ashrams, hospitals, prisons), bed-comforts, charity, sleep, dreams.
**Karaka**: Saturn (losses), Ketu (moksha).
**Key divisional chart**: D-20 (Vimsamsa) for spiritual inclinations; D-9 12th house for moksha (Textbook §34.5, p.457).
**Notes**: a dusthana, but the only house that supports spiritual liberation. A strong 12th in the chart of a spiritual person is a blessing; in the chart of a materialist, a challenge.

## Classification at a glance

| Category | Houses | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| Kendras (angles) | 1, 4, 7, 10 | Structural; planets here gain prominence |
| Trikonas (trines) | 1, 5, 9 | Auspicious; dharma & fortune |
| Dusthanas | 6, 8, 12 | Challenging; but 11th from each carries redemption |
| Upachayas | 3, 6, 10, 11 | Improve over time; welcome malefics |
| Marakas | 2, 7 | Life-shortening (used in longevity analysis, Ch 14) |

Cite the classification itself to Textbook Ch 7 (p.67) when you use it.
