# Dasha analysis — Vimshottari

Textbook Chapter 16, pp.209–225 is the primary source. Rao covers many dasha systems but treats Vimshottari as the default for personal forecasting. Use it as the spine of the reading's forecast section unless the user has explicitly requested another.

## The basic structure

- Vimshottari is a nakshatra-based dasha system. Total cycle: 120 years.
- The nine planets each own a period (Mahadasha) of a fixed length:

  | Planet | Years |
  |---|---|
  | Sun | 6 |
  | Moon | 10 |
  | Mars | 7 |
  | Rahu | 18 |
  | Jupiter | 16 |
  | Saturn | 19 |
  | Mercury | 17 |
  | Ketu | 7 |
  | Venus | 20 |

  Cite `[Textbook Ch 16, p.209]` when referencing the structure.

- Starting dasha is determined by the nakshatra of the natal Moon.
- Each Mahadasha is subdivided into nine Antardashas (sub-periods), each ruled by one of the nine planets in the same order, proportional to that planet's total years. Each Antardasha is further subdivided into Pratyantardashas, etc. For a personal reading, Mahadasha + Antardasha is usually sufficient; Pratyantar is for timing specific events.

## How to interpret a period (Rao's approach)

Rao's method, synthesized from Ch 13 (Interpreting Charts, pp.166–179) and Ch 16:

For the active Mahadasha lord (call it MD):

1. **What does MD signify naturally?** Its karaka roles — e.g., Jupiter is wealth, wisdom, children; Saturn is work, labor, discipline; Venus is love, art, comforts. Cite `[Textbook Ch 8, p.79]`.

2. **What does MD signify in *this* chart?**
   - Which houses does MD **own** (the houses whose sign is ruled by MD)? Those houses' matters become active.
   - Which house does MD **occupy**? Matters of that house rise.
   - Which house is MD the **karaka** for? Same effect.
   - What's MD's **dignity** (exalted/own sign/friendly/debilitated)? A dignified MD delivers results well; a debilitated MD delivers poorly or delays.
   - Any **yogas** MD participates in? Raja yogas fructify; Dhana yogas bring wealth; afflicted combinations bring their difficulties.

3. **How does the Antardasha lord modulate?** The Antardasha (AD) colors the MD. An AD lord that:
   - Shares houses with MD (ownership or placement overlap) → intensifies the MD's themes.
   - Is friendly to MD and well-placed → supports a flavor of MD's natural results.
   - Is inimical to MD or in a dusthana (6/8/12) from MD → creates friction within the MD period.

4. **What houses do MD and AD jointly activate?** Rao's house-activation logic: a period activates houses owned by or occupied by the period lords, and houses aspected by them. Cite `[Textbook Ch 13, p.166]` for the synthesis method and `[Textbook Ch 16, p.209]` for the dasha-specific version.

## Writing the dasha forecast in the PDF

Structure for the forecast section:

### Current Mahadasha: **[Planet] (YYYY–YYYY)**

One paragraph on the Mahadasha lord's general tenor in this chart:
- Natural significations.
- What it owns and occupies; dignity; yoga participation.
- The overall character of this multi-year chapter.

### Current Antardasha: **[Planet] (YYYY-MM to YYYY-MM)**

One paragraph:
- AD lord's significations and placement in this chart.
- How AD colors MD.
- Specific life areas highlighted (the houses AD/MD activate).
- Calibrated likely experiences: "This is a period that tends to bring …"
- If malefic dasha with dusthana involvement, warn with restraint and cite common-sense precautions from §34.1.1.

### Upcoming periods (next 2–3 Antardashas)

Brief paragraphs for each:
- AD lord and approximate date range.
- One-sentence orientation.
- Any specific themes (career peaks, relationship transitions, health vigilance periods).

### Dasha-targeted remedies

Echo the main remedies section but specifically tie remedies to the current dasha lord (e.g., current Saturn MD → Saturn mantra + Saturn-related charity + deity worship of Vishnu/Brahma). Cite Textbook Ch 34 sections.

## Don'ts

- **Don't predict specific events with specific dates.** Rao's system gives periods and themes, not calendar-precision events. Use phrases like "during the middle of this period", "toward the later portion of this Antardasha".
- **Don't predict death from dasha analysis.** Even with Shoola/Ayur Dasa (Textbook Ch 22-23) this requires extreme care and is beyond the scope of a prepared reading.
- **Don't stack multiple dasha systems** in the same forecast unless the user asked. Vimshottari is sufficient for the default reading. Narayana dasha can be added if the user provides it and wants event timing for material areas.

## If the user didn't provide current dasha info

- Explain that without knowing which Mahadasha/Antardasha is currently active, the forecast section will be limited to a general future-trajectory note based on strong natal periods (e.g., "your Jupiter Mahadasha, which covers 16 years starting from [approximate age if Moon nakshatra known], will tend to emphasize …").
- Ask the user to provide the current dasha from their chart software if they want a timing-specific forecast.
